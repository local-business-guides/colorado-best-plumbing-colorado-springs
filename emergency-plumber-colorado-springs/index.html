When to call about an urgent plumbing problem

A plumbing issue that is actively causing damage or leaving an essential fixture unusable deserves prompt attention. Examples can include a pipe that’s releasing water, a toilet that won’t stop overflowing, or a drain problem affecting more than one fixture. If you’re unsure whether the situation is urgent, describe what you’re seeing and ask for guidance rather than guessing at the cause.

If water is spreading, keep away from wet electrical equipment and outlets. You can shut off a fixture’s supply or the home’s main water valve if you know where it is and can reach it safely. What to have ready before a plumber arrives

A few practical details can make it easier to talk through the problem:
- The room or fixture where you first noticed it.
- Whether the water is still running, leaking, or backing up.
- Any steps you’ve already taken, such as turning off a valve.
- A clear path to the affected area, if it’s safe to prepare one.
